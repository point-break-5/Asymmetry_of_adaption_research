## 1. Verified reference

**International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR)** — the single instrument behind all 10 events in this domain.
- **Art. 9** — liberty and security of person. Prohibits arbitrary arrest and detention; any deprivation of liberty must be according to law; anyone arrested must be informed *at the time of arrest* of the reasons and promptly of any charges; detainees may challenge detention before a court (habeas corpus).
- **Art. 14** — fair trial. Equality before courts and tribunals; presumption of innocence; the right to be present at trial; to defend in person or through counsel of one's own choosing; adequate time and facilities to prepare a defence and communicate with counsel; trial without undue delay.
- **Art. 14(7)** — *ne bis in idem* (double jeopardy). The Human Rights Committee has consistently read it as primarily territorial, i.e. it does not by itself bar prosecution in another State.
- **General Comment No. 32 (CCPR/C/GC/32)** is the authoritative interpretation of Art. 14.

## 3. Events and curated ground truth

| ID | Topic | Source instrument | Curated detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CRI_01` | Self-Incrimination | ICCPR | Everyone shall be entitled to minimum guarantees: Not to be compelled to testify against himself or to confess guilt. |
| `CRI_02` | Habeas Corpus | ICCPR | Anyone deprived of liberty by arrest or detention shall be entitled to take proceedings before a court to decide without delay on the lawfulness of detention and order release if it is not lawful |
| `CRI_03` | Presumption of Innocence | ICCPR | Everyone charged with a criminal offence shall have the right to be presumed innocent until proved guilty according to law. |
| `CRI_04` | Double Jeopardy | ICCPR | No one shall be tried or punished again for an offence for which they have been finally convicted or acquitted in accordance with the law and penal procedure of that country (ne bis in idem). |
| `CRI_05` | Right to Counsel | ICCPR | To have adequate time and facilities for the preparation of his defence and to communicate with counsel of his own choosing. |
| `CRI_06` | Humane Treatment | ICCPR | All persons deprived of their liberty shall be treated with humanity and with respect for the inherent dignity of the human person. |
| `CRI_07` | Prompt Trial | ICCPR | Anyone arrested on a criminal charge shall be brought promptly before a judge and entitled to trial within a reasonable time or to release. Pre-trial detention shall not be the general rule; release may be subject to guarantees of appearance. |
| `CRI_08` | Retroactive Laws | ICCPR | No one shall be held guilty of any criminal offence which did not constitute a criminal offence at the time when it was committed. |
| `CRI_09` | Public Hearing | ICCPR | Everyone shall be entitled to a fair and public hearing by a competent, independent and impartial tribunal established by law. |
| `CRI_10` | Free Interpreter | ICCPR | The right to have the free assistance of an interpreter if he cannot understand or speak the language used in court. |
