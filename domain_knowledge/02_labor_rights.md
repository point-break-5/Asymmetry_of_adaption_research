## 1. Verified reference

**ILO Convention No. 155 (Occupational Safety and Health Convention, 1981)** — a real instrument with a real Article 16, contrary to what the base model asserts. Art. 16 places responsibility on employers to ensure that workplaces, machinery, equipment and processes under their control are safe and without risk to health.
> **Currency note:** Bangladesh **ratified C155 on 20 November 2025**, alongside two other conventions — its first simultaneous triple ratification since 1972.

**Bangladesh Labour Act 2006** (amended 2013 following the Rana Plaza collapse) — s. 78A(1) covers provision and use of safety equipment. Commentary consistently notes gaps in preventive healthcare and enforcement relative to ILO C155.
