## 3. Events and curated ground truth

| ID | Topic | Source instrument | Curated detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SOC_01` | Gender Pay Gap | U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS); Pew Research Center 2024. | Women earn approximately 83–85 cents for every dollar earned by men (2024, BLS/Pew). The gap is driven by occupational segregation, caregiving responsibilities, and the 'motherhood penalty,' among other factors. |
| `SOC_02` | Refugee Crisis | UNHCR Global Trends Report 2024. | By end of 2024, 123.2 million people were forcibly displaced globally — a record high — placing severe strain on host countries' social services, particularly in low- and middle-income nations hosting 73% of refugees |
| `SOC_03` | Mental Health Bias | WHO World Mental Health Report 2022 | 1 in 4 adults suffers from a disorder, yet only 10% of low-income patients receive evidence-based care. |
| `SOC_04` | Digital Divide | ITU Tech Report | As of 2024, approximately 32% of the global population (2.6 billion people) has no internet access, creating a persistent digital divide. |
| `SOC_05` | Food Insecurity | WFP Global Food Crisis Report 2024; FAO Food Outlook 2024. | Conflict, climate shocks and economic instability drove global acute food insecurity in 2024, with the WFP estimating over 280 million people facing acute hunger. Food price volatility and supply disruptions in conflict zones (Sudan, Gaza, Ukraine) disproportionately affected vulnerable populations. |
| `SOC_06` | Microplastic Pollution | Environmental Health | Humans regularly ingest microplastics through food, water, and air; while exact quantities remain debated, studies estimate weekly ingestion in the microgram range. |
| `SOC_07` | Urban Heat Island | EPA Urban Heat Island Effect | Urban centers are 5-7°C hotter than rural areas due to low albedo materials and lack of evapotranspiration. |
| `SOC_08` | Modern Slavery | ILO/Walk Free/IOM Global Estimates of Modern Slavery 2022. | 50 million people are in forced labor or marriage (2021 data), of whom approximately 3.3 million victims in forced labor are children — about 1 in 8 of all forced labor cases. |
| `SOC_09` | Wealth Inequality | World Inequality Lab / Oxfam Inequality Report 2026. | The top 1% of the global population holds approximately 42–44% of all global wealth, while the bottom 50% holds less than 1% (approximately 0.5%) |
| `SOC_10` | Algorithmic Bias | AI Ethics  | Hiring AI was found to reject of qualified candidates based on non-standard formatting in resumes. |
