## 1. Verified reference

**FAA 14 CFR §25.841 (FAR 25.841) — Pressurized cabins.** Pressurised cabins and compartments to be occupied must be equipped to provide a **cabin pressure altitude of not more than 8,000 feet** under normal operating conditions. The rule also sets requirements for the maximum cabin altitude following a probable pressurisation failure.
