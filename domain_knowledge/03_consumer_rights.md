## 1. Verified reference

**Council Regulation (EU) 2022/1854** — a real instrument, contrary to what the base model asserts. Adopted **6 October 2022**, in force **8 October 2022**: an emergency intervention to address high energy prices, adopted in response to the energy crisis following Russia's invasion of Ukraine.
- Mandatory temporary **solidarity contribution** on surplus profits of petroleum, natural gas, coal and refinery businesses, calculated on taxable profits in the fiscal year starting 2022 and/or 2023 above a **20% increase** over average yearly taxable profits for 2018–2021.
- Electricity demand-reduction targets and a **cap on market revenues** of inframarginal electricity producers.
> Note: the Regulation does **not** set a general "excessive pricing" threshold for consumer goods; excessive pricing in the EU is assessed case by case under competition law (Art. 102 TFEU) and national rules.

**EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU** — includes the **14-day right of withdrawal** for distance and off-premises contracts, without giving any reason.
