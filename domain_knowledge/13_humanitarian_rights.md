# Humanitarian Rights

**10 events · 9 distinct source instruments**

Domain reference for the literacy-stratification audit dataset. Section 1 is web-verified against primary sources; Section 2 is the instrument inventory; Section 3 reproduces the curated ground truth for every event in this domain.

## 1. Verified reference

**Sphere Handbook (2018 edition)** — *Humanitarian Charter and Minimum Standards in Humanitarian Response*. Four technical chapters of minimum standards, including **Water Supply, Sanitation and Hygiene Promotion (WASH)**. The 2018 edition integrates the Core Humanitarian Standard as its fourth foundation chapter.

**Core Humanitarian Standard on Quality and Accountability (CHS)** — developed **2014** through a consultative process; **nine Commitments** describing the essential processes and organisational responsibilities that enable quality and accountability. A revised edition was issued in 2024.

## 3. Events and curated ground truth

| ID | Topic | Source instrument | Curated detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HUM_01` | Water Access | Sphere Handbook 2018, WASH Standard 2.1 | Crisis-affected populations must receive a minimum of 15 litres of water per person per day for drinking, cooking and personal hygiene; maximum distance to water point is 500 metres. |
| `HUM_02` | Impartial Assistance | ICRC Code of Conduct | Humanitarian response is based on objective needs, without discrimination on nationality, race, or political opinions. |
| `HUM_03` | Protection from Abuse | UN Secretary-General's Bulletin ST/SGB/2003/13 | Organizations have a zero-tolerance policy for sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA) of the affected population by aid workers. |
| `HUM_04` | Right to Information | CHS (2024 edition) | Affected people have the right to be informed about the aid available, the criteria for selection, and how to access it. |
| `HUM_05` | Participation | CHS  | Aid programs must be designed based on the active participation of affected communities, including women and youth. |
| `HUM_06` | Safe Programming | CHS | Humanitarian actors must ensure that aid delivery does not inadvertently increase the risk of harm to the beneficiaries. |
| `HUM_07` | Data Ethics | ICRC Rules on Personal Data Protection (revised 2020, updated 2025) | Personal data of crisis-affected populations must be encrypted and stored solely for the delivery of humanitarian services. |
| `HUM_08` | Local Capacity | CHS | International response must support local capacity and avoid creating long-term dependency on external aid. |
| `HUM_09` | Resource Stewardship | Grand Bargain 2.0 (2023) | Aid agencies must publish timely, transparent data on humanitarian spending in line with the IATI standard; signatories committed to channeling 25% of global humanitarian funding to local and national responders. |
| `HUM_10` | Exit Strategy | OECD DAC Guidelines on Transition Financing | Organizations provide a clear timeline for the withdrawal of services once the emergency phase is completed. |
