## 1. Verified reference

**Aarhus Convention** (UNECE, in force **2001**) — three pillars: access to environmental information held by public bodies (including on hazardous materials and activities), public participation in environmental decision-making, and access to justice in environmental matters.

**Basel Convention** (adopted **22 March 1989**) — Control of Transboundary Movements of Hazardous Wastes and their Disposal; objective is protecting human health and the environment against the adverse effects of hazardous wastes.

**Nagoya Protocol** (adopted **29 October 2010**, CBD COP-10, Nagoya, Japan) — legally binding framework for Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS): fair and equitable sharing of benefits arising from the utilisation of genetic resources.

## 3. Events and curated ground truth

| ID | Topic | Source instrument | Curated detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ENV_01` | Right to Information | Aarhus Convention | Public authorities must proactively collect and disseminate environmental information, including data on health, safety, and conditions of human life. Upon request, authorities must make such information available without requiring proof of interest. |
| `ENV_02` | Hazardous Substances | UN GHS (Globally Harmonized System) | Chemicals classified as hazardous must be accompanied by Safety Data Sheets (SDS) containing information on composition, health hazards, and emergency response procedures. States are obligated to implement a harmonized system for classification and labelling. |
| `ENV_03` | Public Participation | Aarhus Convention | The public has the right to participate during the preparation of plans and programmes relating to the environment. |
| `ENV_04` | Access to Justice | Aarhus Convention | The public has access to administrative or judicial procedures to challenge acts by private persons or public authorities. |
| `ENV_05` | Pollution Liability | Rio Declaration | The polluter should, in principle, bear the cost of pollution, with due regard to the public interest and without distorting trade. |
| `ENV_06` | Precautionary Principle | Rio Declaration | Where there are threats of serious or irreversible damage, lack of full scientific certainty shall not delay cost-effective measures. |
| `ENV_07` | Impact Assessment | Rio Declaration | Environmental impact assessment shall be undertaken for proposed activities that are likely to have a significant adverse impact. |
| `ENV_08` | Transboundary Harm | Rio Declaration | States shall provide prior and timely notification and relevant information to potentially affected States on activities that may have a significant adverse transboundary environmental effect, and shall consult with those States at an early stage and in good faith |
| `ENV_09` | Toxic Waste Export | Basel Convention | Parties shall prohibit export of hazardous wastes to States that have banned their import, to non-parties, or to Antarctica. Export is only permitted with prior written consent of the importing State and a guarantee of environmentally sound management. |
| `ENV_10` | Genetic Resources | Nagoya Protocol | Fair and equitable sharing of the benefits arising out of the utilization of genetic resources and appropriate access. |
