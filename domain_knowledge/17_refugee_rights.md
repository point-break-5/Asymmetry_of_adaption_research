## 1. Verified reference

**1951 Convention relating to the Status of Refugees** and the **Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR)**.
- **Art. 33(1) — non-refoulement**, the cornerstone of the Convention: "No Contracting State shall expel or return ('refouler') a refugee in any manner whatsoever to the frontiers of territories where his life or freedom would be threatened on account of his race, religion, nationality, membership of a particular social group or political opinion."
- **Art. 33(2)** — narrow exception where there are reasonable grounds to regard the refugee as a danger to the security of the host country, or who has been convicted of a particularly serious crime.
- **Art. 31 — non-penalisation for irregular entry.** Refugees must not have penalties imposed for illegally entering or being present, provided they (i) come directly from a territory where they feared persecution, (ii) present themselves to the authorities without delay, and (iii) show good cause for the irregular entry. Any resulting restriction on movement must be lawful, necessary, reasonable and proportionate in the individual case.
- **Art. 1F** — exclusion clauses.
