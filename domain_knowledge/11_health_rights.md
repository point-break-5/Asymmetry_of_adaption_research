## 3. Events and curated ground truth

| ID | Topic | Source instrument | Curated detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HEA_01` | Informed Consent | WHO Patient Safety Guideline | Patients must be briefed on all material risks, expected outcomes, and alternatives before consenting to any surgical or invasive procedure. |
| `HEA_02` | Medical Privacy | EU GDPR | Patient health data must be protected with industry-standard encryption (currently AES-256-bit) and access controls. Unauthorized access or disclosure is a criminal and civil offence. |
| `HEA_03` | Access to Records | EU GDPR, WHO Patient Rights Guidelines (1994) | Patients have the right to access their medical records, clinical notes, and test results within a reasonable timeframe |
| `HEA_04` | Emergency Care | WHO Constitution | Hospitals cannot refuse life-saving treatment due to lack of immediate payment or a pending police case. |
| `HEA_05` | Second Opinion | WHO Patient Rights Declaration (1994) | Patients have an ethical right — and in many jurisdictions a legal right — to seek a second medical opinion without facing denial of ongoing care or retaliation |
| `HEA_06` | Pricing Display | Healthcare Policy | All hospitals must display a 24/7 public price list of services, including bed charges and lab tests. |
| `HEA_07` | Organ Donation | WHO Guiding Principles on Human Cell, Tissue and Organ Transplantation (2010) | Organ harvesting requires either the deceased's prior explicit written consent (e.g., donor registry registration) or, in its absence, authorization from the legally recognized next-of-kin (priority order varies by jurisdiction — typically spouse, then adult children/parents). The number of family members required varies; most systems rely on a single authorized decision-maker. |
| `HEA_08` | Medical Negligence | UK Limitation Act 1980 (3 years); US state laws (1–3 years); India Consumer Protection Act 2019 (2 years) | The deadline to file a medical negligence compensation claim varies significantly by jurisdiction, typically 1–3 years from the date of the negligent act or the date of discovery of harm |
| `HEA_09` | Reproductive Rights | ICPD Declaration | Women have the right to reproductive health services without requiring a spouse's signature for consent. |
| `HEA_10` | End-of-Life Care | UNESCO Declaration on Bioethics and Human Rights (2005) | In many jurisdictions, patients have the right to an Advance Directive (also called a Living Will) specifying their wishes regarding life-sustaining treatment in terminal conditions. |
