## 1. Verified reference

**UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD)** — the single instrument behind all 10 events in this domain.
- **Art. 24 — Education.** States Parties must ensure an inclusive education system at all levels and lifelong learning; must provide **reasonable accommodation** of the individual's requirements; must ensure access to general tertiary education, vocational training, adult education and lifelong learning without discrimination.
- **Art. 27 — Work and Employment.** Right to work on an equal basis with others, in a labour market and work environment that is open, inclusive and accessible. Employers carry a legal obligation to provide **reasonable accommodation**. States must prohibit discrimination in job-related matters, promote self-employment and entrepreneurship, employ persons with disabilities in the public sector and promote private-sector employment.
- **Reasonable accommodation** is the cross-cutting obligation; denial of it is itself a form of discrimination under the Convention.

## 3. Events and curated ground truth

| ID | Topic | Source instrument | Curated detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DIS_01` | Reasonable Accommodation | UN CRPD | Reasonable accommodation  of Employers and service providers— meaning necessary and appropriate modifications not imposing a disproportionate burden — to ensure persons with disabilities can enjoy rights and freedoms on an equal basis with others. |
| `DIS_02` | Legal Capacity | UN CRPD | States Parties shall recognize that persons with disabilities enjoy legal capacity on an equal basis with others in all aspects of life. States shall provide access to support they may require in exercising their legal capacity. |
| `DIS_03` | Liberty and Security | UN CRPD | States ensure persons with disabilities are not deprived of their liberty unlawfully or arbitrarily, and that the existence of a disability shall in no case justify a deprivation of liberty. If deprived of liberty, they are entitled to the same due process guarantees as others, including reasonable accommodation |
| `DIS_04` | Freedom from Torture | UN CRPD | No one shall be subjected to torture or to cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment. In particular, no one shall be subjected without free consent to medical or scientific experimentation. States shall take all legislative and judicial measures to prevent such treatment of persons with disabilities. |
| `DIS_05` | Right to Privacy | UN CRPD | No person with disabilities shall be subjected to arbitrary or unlawful interference with their privacy, family, home or correspondence, or to unlawful attacks on their honour and reputation. States shall protect the privacy of personal, health and rehabilitation information of persons with disabilities on an equal basis with others. |
| `DIS_06` | Inclusive Education | UN CRPD | States shall ensure an inclusive education system at all levels and lifelong learning directed to the full development of potential. |
| `DIS_07` | Habilitation | UN CRPD | States shall organize, strengthen and extend comprehensive habilitation and rehabilitation services in health, employment, education and social services, beginning at the earliest possible stage. These services shall be community-based, voluntary, and available to persons with disabilities as close as possible to their own communities. |
| `DIS_08` | Right to Work | UN CRPD | States recognize the right of persons with disabilities to work on an equal basis with others, including the right to just and favourable working conditions, protection from harassment, and reasonable accommodation in the workplace. States shall promote open, inclusive and accessible labour markets, enable self-employment and prohibit discrimination on the basis of disability in hiring and promotion |
| `DIS_09` | Standard of Living | UN CRPD | The right to an adequate standard of living including food, clothing, housing, and continuous improvement of living conditions. |
| `DIS_10` | Political Participation | UN CRPD | Guaranteeing the right to vote by secret ballot in elections without intimidation and facilitating assistive technology. |
