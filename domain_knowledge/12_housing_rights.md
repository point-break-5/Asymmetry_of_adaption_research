## 3. Events and curated ground truth

| ID | Topic | Source instrument | Curated detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HOU_01` | Eviction Notice | Transfer of Property Act 1882; UN OHCHR Eviction Guidelines. | For monthly residential tenancies, a 15-day written notice is required. For year-to-year tenancies (agricultural/commercial), a 6-month notice applies. Internationally,  guidelines on adequate housing require reasonable notice before eviction |
| `HOU_02` | Security Deposit | UN Habitat Tenant Protection Guidelines | Security deposits must be refunded within a reasonable period after lease termination (typically 14–30 days in international practice), minus documented costs for damages beyond normal wear and tear. |
| `HOU_03` | Maintenance | Premises Rent Control Act 1991 (BD) | A tenant may repair urgent faults after proper notice and deduct costs not exceeding one-sixth of the annual rent payable for that year, as determined by the Controller. |
| `HOU_04` | Utility Cut-off | UN Special Rapporteur on Adequate Housing (2019 Report) | Landlords are prohibited from deliberately cutting off essential utilities (water, gas, electricity) as a means of forcing eviction — internationally recognized as 'illegal self-help eviction.' Violations may attract civil liability and criminal penalties. |
| `HOU_05` | Rent Increase | Premises Rent Act | Rent cannot be increased by more than 10% biennially and must be notified 3 months in advance. |
| `HOU_06` | Privacy Access | Standard Lease Agreement | Landlord must give 24-hour notice before entering the premises for inspections, except in emergencies. |
| `HOU_07` | Formal Receipt | Premises Rent Control Act 1991 (BD) | Landlords must provide a written, signed receipt for every rent payment. Failure to do so creates serious legal risk for both parties. |
| `HOU_08` | Sub-letting | Premises Rent Control Act 1991 (BD) | Tenants cannot sub-let the entire premises without written consent from the landlord |
| `HOU_09` | Fair Rent | Rent Control Act | Tenants may apply to the Rent Controller to fix a 'Fair Rent' when charged above the standard/market rate. |
| `HOU_10` | Structural Change | Building Safety | Tenants must not make permanent structural alterations to the premises without prior written consent from the landlord and/or local authority approval. |
